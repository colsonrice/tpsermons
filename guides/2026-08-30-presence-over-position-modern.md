# Presence Over Position

The Urgent Kingdom · Mark 9:30-50

[Message page](https://tpcc.org/messages/presence-over-position) · [Watch](https://youtu.be/-F6w9h2Jpg8) · [Listen](https://episodes.captivate.fm/episode/0eb08548-738f-4add-aada-4483d1fe12d9.mp3)

**At a glance.** About 60 minutes, 10 to 12 men, Bibles and a pen. To challenge men to prioritize the presence of Jesus over their personal ambitions, applying this mindset to their daily lives.

---

## Before You Begin

The sermon explores Mark 9:30-50, focusing on the disciples’ misunderstanding of greatness and Jesus' teaching on true significance. The core tension is the human tendency to equate greatness with position, while Jesus presents a counter-cultural vision focusing on servanthood. The preacher uses Teddy Roosevelt's story as an illustration of personal formation through hardship. This parallels the disciples’ journey as they struggle with their own ambitions.

The sermon outlines four principles from Jesus’ teaching: serve without an agenda, surrender the impulse to be territorial, stop tolerating sin, and stay salty as preservatives of the kingdom. These principles counter the disciples' misunderstanding of greatness and challenge us to reconsider how we define influence and success. This is not about removing ambition but redirecting it towards godly ends.

Pastoral sensitivities in the sermon include dealing with personal sin, feeling stuck in life's difficult seasons (Badlands), and the struggle with placing too much emphasis on position over presence. These challenge the group to reflect on their current life situations and how they are relating to God and others amid these challenges.

## The True Measure of Greatness

In this section, Jesus challenges the disciples on their understanding of greatness by placing a child before them. He illustrates that welcoming the seemingly insignificant is a mark of true greatness. This flips worldly definitions of success, calling us to serve without expecting anything in return.

**Who have you overlooked in your life that you could serve without expectation?**

**How has focusing on personal ambition affected your relationships?**

**Where have you seen God use a humble act of service to impact others?**

**What does serving without recognition reveal about your heart's motives?**

*If it stalls:* Think of someone specific in your life who seems insignificant. When has ambition led you away from meaningful connections?

---

## Surrendering Territorialism

The disciples struggled with territorialism, demonstrated by their reaction to someone casting out demons in Jesus' name. Jesus corrects them, highlighting that we're part of a larger mission not confined by our limited views. It's about advancing the Kingdom, not guarding our influence.

**Where have you been territorial in your spiritual life or relationships?**

**How can you support others in their godly pursuits, even if they don’t align with your preferences?**

**What keeps you from celebrating others’ successes as part of God’s Kingdom work?**

*If it stalls:* Think of a time when you've seen division among believers. Recall a moment when you felt left out of God's work.

---

## Cutting Off Sin

Jesus uses stark imagery to stress the seriousness of sin, urging radical action against anything that leads us away from God. The metaphor of severing a limb reveals the desperate need for purity and commitment to holiness. It highlights the reality of sin’s potential to destroy if not dealt with.

**What sin have you tolerated or justified that needs to be cut off today?**

**When have you underestimated the cost of sin in your life?**

**How can you create a plan to actively cut out sin and its triggers?**

**Who can you invite into your journey of eliminating this sin?**

*If it stalls:* Identify a pattern or habit that you've downplayed. Consider what it might actually cost you if left unchecked.

---

## Staying Salty

Jesus concludes with the need to stay 'salty', to preserve faith and values in a decaying world. This involves living distinctively, upholding truth, and exuding the characteristics of God’s Kingdom in our daily actions. We’re called to be agents of change through our counter-cultural lifestyles.

**How can your everyday actions reflect Kingdom values?**

**What challenges your distinctiveness as a Christian in your environment?**

**In what ways can you be a 'preservative' in your community or workplace?**

*If it stalls:* What specific habits set you apart or blend you in? Who could you influence positively by being distinctively Christ-like?

## Key Themes to Reinforce

- True greatness is about servanthood, not position.
- Spiritual growth often involves enduring and learning from hardships.
- Territorialism hinders collective Kingdom work.
- Sin must be addressed decisively to align with God’s formation process.
- Being 'salty' means living distinctively to preserve Kingdom values.
