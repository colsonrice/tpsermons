# The Fragile Covenant

*Reflection sheet*

The Urgent Kingdom · Mark 10 · Aaron Brockett, Lead Pastor

---

## Questioning Marriage

1. **How has my family's story shaped my view of marriage, and how have I seen God's grace in that?**
2. **When have I faced questions about my faith with mixed motives, and how did I respond?**
3. **Where have I seen hard-heartedness impact my relationships, and what steps am I taking to soften my heart?**
4. **Have I felt overwhelmed by relational failure, and how did I hold onto hope?**

## Marriage's Purpose

5. **Where do I see marriage as a covenant, not a contract, despite societal messages?**
6. **How does seeing marriage as a reflection of Christ's commitment challenge how I invest in relationships?**
7. **Where have I felt spiritual opposition in my relationships, and how am I fighting it?**
8. **Reflect on a time I settled for less in a relationship. What did I learn about God's designs?**
9. **How am I nurturing oneness in my relationships, or where might I need to grow?**

## Hope for the Broken

10. **What past failures haunt me, and where do I need to see God's grace bringing redemption?**
11. **Have I experienced God's grace rewriting my story, and what impact has that had on my relationships?**
12. **How can I uphold the value of marriage in my current or future relationships despite cultural pressures?**
13. **Where am I currently struggling to see hope, and how does Jesus's example challenge that view?**
