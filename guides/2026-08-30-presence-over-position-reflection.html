Presence Over Position

Reflection sheet

The Urgent Kingdom · Mark 9:30-50

Presence over Position

  1. Where in my life have I pursued recognition over presence?
  2. Think about a time I served someone with no expectation of return. What did that reveal about my motives?
  3. How do I balance ambition with the call to serve humbly?

Surrendering Control

  1. When have I felt the need to control or judge someone's spiritual journey?
  2. How do I handle feelings of jealousy or competition in my spiritual life?
  3. What steps can I take to be more inclusive in my faith community?

Dealing with Sin Seriously

  1. What 'small sins' have I been tolerating that could grow into larger issues?
  2. Think about a temptation I face. How can I address it in a constructive way?
  3. In what ways has ignoring sin affected my personal or spiritual growth?

Staying Salty

  1. In what environments do I find it hardest to maintain my Christian identity?
  2. How do I remain 'salty' in situations that challenge my values?
  3. What practices help reinforce my faith when external pressures mount?

This Week

Decide one way to prioritize presence with God and serving others, sharing your plan with a group member for accountability.

Put it in a note on your phone, then text it to the man who will ask you about it next week.
