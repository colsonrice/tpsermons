The Urgent Kingdom

Presence Over Position

Reflection sheet

The Urgent Kingdom · Mark 9:30-50

Arguing Greatness

  1. When have I found myself seeking position over experiencing presence, and what was the impact on me?
  2. Identify a recent instance where I served without seeking recognition. How did it affect me?
  3. Think of a time I welcomed someone who could offer me nothing in return. What did I learn from it?
  4. What current pursuit of position might be distracting me from truly trusting Jesus now?

Handling Sin and Temptation

  1. Reflect on a sin I've been managing instead of addressing directly. What step can I take to 'cut it off'?
  2. Where have I underestimated the impact of a 'manageable' sin in my life, and what has been the cost?
  3. How does understanding the consequences of sin challenge me to examine my life differently?
  4. What immediate steps can I take today to remove a sin or influence that diverges me from God?

True Greatness

  1. In what area of my life can I serve without expectation of return this week?
  2. How can I begin to shift my mindset from competition to partnership in my ministry or work environment?
  3. Where is God calling me to be a distinctive preservative, influencing without watering down my faith?
  4. When am I most tempted to pursue personal success over God's calling, and what can I do to shift this?

This Week

Decide on one specific area this week where you will choose presence over position and ask another man to check in with you before next meeting.

Put it in a note on your phone, then text it to the man who will ask you about it next week.
