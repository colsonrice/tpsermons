# Presence Over Position

The Urgent Kingdom · Mark 9:30-50

---

## The True Measure of Greatness

**Who have I overlooked in my life that I could serve without expectation?**



**How has focusing on personal ambition affected my relationships?**



**Where have I seen God use a humble act of service to impact others?**



**What does serving without recognition reveal about my heart's motives?**



## Surrendering Territorialism

**Where have I been territorial in my spiritual life or relationships?**



**How can I support others in their godly pursuits, even if they don’t align with my preferences?**



**What keeps me from celebrating others’ successes as part of God’s Kingdom work?**



## Cutting Off Sin

**What sin have I tolerated or justified that needs to be cut off today?**



**When have I underestimated the cost of sin in my life?**



**How can I create a plan to actively cut out sin and its triggers?**



**Who can I invite into my journey of eliminating this sin?**



## Staying Salty

**How can my everyday actions reflect Kingdom values?**



**What challenges my distinctiveness as a Christian in my environment?**



**In what ways can I be a 'preservative' in my community or workplace?**

Decide on one person you will serve this week without expecting anything in return, and share your commitment with a group member for accountability.
