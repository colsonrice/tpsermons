2The Rich Young Ruler

SayLet's start by looking at the rich young ruler, a man who ran to Jesus but couldn't commit fully.

Background

The rich young ruler approached Jesus, seeking eternal life, but had a glaring attachment. He was successful yet couldn't part with his wealth, revealing the one thing he held above all. Jesus loved him enough to tell him what he didn't want to hear.

  1. The ruler ran to Jesus seeking assurance of eternal life.

    What drove you to your last encounter with God asking for assurance?

    Follow upIs there a recurring insecurity driving your requests?

  2. Must ask

    Despite his wealth, the ruler lacked something crucial.

    What is your 'one thing' you fear would break you if lost?

    Follow upWhen did you first realize this could be your 'one thing'?

  3. Jesus loved the ruler enough to tell him hard truths.

    Who last told hard truth to you because they love you?

    Follow upHow did you initially react, and what have you done since?

  4. The ruler wanted an achievement-based answer from Jesus.

    When have you mistaken action for assurance in your spiritual life?

    Follow upHow did you recognize the difference?

3Jesus' Challenge and Love

SayJesus presented both challenge and love to the ruler, showing genuine concern for his soul above all else.

Background

Jesus noted that the kingdom is a gift to be received, not achieved. Yet, he laid a direct challenge before the ruler that was too costly in the young man's eyes. His possessions held him more than he held them.

  1. Must ask

    The kingdom of God is to be received, like a child.

    What aspect of your faith do you still approach as if you need to achieve something?

    Follow upHow does this affect your view of God's grace?

  2. Jesus proposed a radical step to the ruler.

    Has God ever asked you to take a step that seemed too big at the time?

    Follow upHow did you rationalize any resistance?

  3. The young ruler left saddened by Jesus' request.

    When have you walked away from a challenge with God, and did you revisit the decision?

    Follow upWhat emotion surrounded your decision at that time?

  4. Jesus extends love even while challenging the ruler.

    Who in your life do you find difficult to lovingly challenge, and why?

    Follow upWhat would make it easier to have that conversation?

4The Call to Surrender

SaySurrendering fully to God means letting go of our functional saviors, those false securities we cling to.

Background

Jesus invites us to let go of what we've made ultimate, surrender, and trust him as our true provider. Our attachments can blind us to God's greater purpose, but surrender leads to true freedom.

  1. Must ask

    To follow Christ is to surrender completely.

    What's one area you hesitate to let Christ rule?

    Follow upWhat's at stake for you in this area?

  2. Faithful, consistent generosity can break idolatry.

    How do you practice generosity in a way that stretches you?

    Follow upDoes your current approach truly challenge your comfort?

  3. Attachment to possessions can rightly orient our hearts.

    What possession or status do you sense God challenging you to hold loosely now?

    Follow upWhat would holding it loosely change in your life?

  4. Jesus challenges us to question our securities.

    What securities in your life do you prioritize over your relationship with God?

    Follow upWhat patterns emerge when these are threatened?

If the room...

Someone is quiet about a personal 'one thing'.

Ask if there's anything they feel called to share, no pressure, just openness.

Discussion sways to financial status as defining success.

Recenter around what Jesus defines as true riches: relationship with Him.

Someone justifies holding onto a possession tightly.

Gently ask what peace those possessions actually bring compared to God.

A participant minimizes their role in God's kingdom.

Affirm their unique place in the church community, noting their gifts.

A man doubts any significant change from surrender.

Share a story of transformation through surrender and God's faithfulness.

Steer by these themes
  • Surrender over achievement.
  • Faith as a gift, not an accomplishment.
  • True wealth is counted in God's kingdom.
  • Attachment vs. discipleship.
  • Generosity breaks the hold of idolatry.
  • Jesus' love in truth-telling.
  • The challenge of comfort zones.
