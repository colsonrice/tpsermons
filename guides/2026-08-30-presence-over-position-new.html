2Serve Without an Agenda

SayLet's talk about what it means to serve without looking for return or recognition.

Background

Jesus emphasized serving others without seeking personal gain. He used a child, who can't repay, as the perfect illustration. Serving should be without expectation of return.

  1. Must ask

    Jesus highlighted serving with no expectation of return.

    When was the last time you gave or served without expecting anything in return?

    Follow upWhat did you feel at that moment, and was it different from serving with an agenda?

  2. Being positioned last embraces countercultural service.

    Recall a time you chose to seek another's success over your own. How did it change you?

    Follow upWhat did this reveal about your motives?

  3. The call to embrace serving others.

    Identify an area in your life where you resist being last. How can you change this?

    Follow upHow does fear of losing position feed into this?

  4. Ambition redirected towards godly goals.

    How can you transform personal ambition into godly ambition in everyday life?

    Follow upWhat is one ambition you can redirect this week?

3Surrender Territorial Impulses

SayNow we'll consider the challenge of letting go of our need to control and dominate spaces.

Background

Jesus spoke against being territorial, highlighting unity over division. He wants us to work in harmony, even across differences, without competing or claiming exclusive rights.

  1. Jesus encouraged unity over division.

    When have you felt threatened by someone crossing into your territory at work or home?

    Follow upWhat did you do with those feelings?

  2. Must ask

    Territorial thinking can divide us.

    Think of a time when cooperation yielded better results than competition. How did it alter your approach?

    Follow upWhat was your biggest learning from that experience?

  3. Unity beyond our comfort zones.

    How do you typically respond to someone with a different approach or belief system?

    Follow upWhere does this response need to change?

  4. John's defensive reaction to outsiders.

    Reflect on a past incident where you guarded your group's 'insider' status. What motivated this?

    Follow upWhat would Jesus say to you about this boundary?

4Stop Tolerating Sin

SayLet's dig into the weighty matter of sin and how Jesus calls us to cut it off at the root.

Background

Jesus uses stark imagery to convey the urgent need to deal with sin radically. He warns against complacency and emphasizes the severe consequences of unchecked sin, urging us towards purity.

  1. Radical action against sin is necessary.

    What sin or bad habit have you been managing instead of removing?

    Follow upHow does this management affect your spiritual life?

  2. Must ask

    Sin always introduces itself as harmless.

    Can you remember a slip into sin that seemed minor but escalated?

    Follow upWhat changes might prevent a repeat?

  3. Sin's lurking danger beneath the surface.

    Identify something in your life that might be a spiritual threat. How will you address it?

    Follow upWhat first step can you take this week to address it?

  4. The metaphor of cutting off sin highlights its seriousness.

    Reflect on an area where you've compromised morality. How will you repent and redirect now?

    Follow upWhat's the smallest step towards repentance you can make in the next few days?
