2Serve Without Agenda

SayJesus doesn't just compete for positions of power, he flips the script, calling us to serve without expectations.

Background

In Mark 9, Jesus challenges His disciples' debates over greatness by placing a child, a symbol of humility and service, before them. Recognizing true greatness requires a redirection of ambition from self-serving to serving others.

  1. Must ask

    Jesus used a child to illustrate ultimate humility and service.

    Who in your life defines your concept of service without agenda?

    Follow upHow have they changed your approach to serving others?

  2. Our culture often equates greatness with visibility.

    When have you chosen a less visible role and found it fulfilling?

    Follow upWhat made the experience rewarding given low visibility?

  3. A child in Jesus’ time had no legal standing.

    How do you treat people who hold no power over you?

    Follow upWhat changes could you make to value them more intentionally?

  4. The preacher noted those sacrificial actions without expectation.

    Where do you still find yourself expecting something in return?

    Follow upWhat changes in your heart when things are given freely?

3Surrender the Impulse to be Territorial

SayThe disciples tried to shut down others who weren't in their 'group'. Jesus corrected them, pointing toward a broader kingdom vision.

Background

The sermon outlined territorial behavior that limits God's work. Jesus rebukes the disciples for trying to control who can operate in His name, indicating a call for unity and cooperation within the church.

  1. Must ask

    John wanted to stop others from using Jesus' name.

    When have you put up walls around your faith?

    Follow upWhat drove you to guard rather than share your faith?

  2. Territorial attitudes limit our vision of God’s work.

    Where are you holding control that needs release?

    Follow upWhat patterns do you notice when you're protective?

  3. Jesus emphasized working together in unity.

    Have you experienced unity bringing a breakthrough?

    Follow upWhat changed when you chose unity over division?

  4. The disciples feared losing their position.

    When have you experienced jealousy over someone else's role?

    Follow upWhat was the underlying fear or insecurity?

4Avoid Sin, Stay Salty

SayJesus calls us to purity and impact, avoiding sin but seasoning life with godly characteristics.

Background

Through hyperbolic language, Jesus illustrates the drastic measures sometimes necessary to avoid sin, emphasizing personal integrity and a life that influences others imperatively and positively.

  1. Must ask

    Cutting off sin is about drastic measures for purity.

    Where are you tolerating a sin that needs to be addressed?

    Follow upWhat is the first small step you can take towards change?

  2. Jesus warned of losing saltiness, meaning influence.

    How have you maintained or lost your Christian influence?

    Follow upWhat factors helped you stay or lose effectiveness as salt during life's challenges?

  3. Salt preserves, not just flavors our actions in society.

    What prevents you from being a preservative in difficult environments?

    Follow upHow can you lean more into showing up as a godly influence?

  4. Jesus talked about avoiding anything that leads us astray.

    When have you ignored a warning and faced consequences?

    Follow upHow could awareness lead to a better response in the future?

6Go around and pray

Steer by these themes
  • Emphasize presence over position in our spiritual journey.
  • Genuine greatness is found in serving others selflessly.
  • Territorial impulses limit God's work; embrace unity.
  • Avoid sin passionately, illustrated through decisive, corrective action.
  • Live with kingdom influence, be salty and distinctive as Christ commands.
  • Personal and spiritual formation is prioritized over situation resolution.
  • Heeding the transformative lessons in life’s challenging 'Badlands'.
