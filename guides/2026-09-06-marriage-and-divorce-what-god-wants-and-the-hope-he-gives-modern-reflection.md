# Marriage and Divorce: What God Wants and the Hope He Gives

The Urgent Kingdom · Mark 10 · Aaron Brockett

---

## Marriage Is God's Idea

**Where have I settled for less than God's design in my relationships?**



**When have I prioritized personal desires over commitment, and what was the outcome?**



**Who models godly commitment in my life, and what have I learned from them?**



**What steps can I take to strengthen my role in a key relationship?**



## Marriage as a Covenant

**Where have I treated relationships as transactional rather than covenantal?**



**When did I extend grace beyond what was deserved, and what did that teach me about covenant?**



**How has my understanding of commitment changed my approach to difficulties?**



**What examples from my life illustrate living out a covenantal relationship?**



## Grace and Redemption

**Where do I need redemption in my relationships?**



**How has an experience of grace transformed my perspective on past mistakes?**



**Which relationship in my life needs grace right now, and what could change if I offered it?**



**How can I support others in seeking redemption and healing in their relational struggles?**
