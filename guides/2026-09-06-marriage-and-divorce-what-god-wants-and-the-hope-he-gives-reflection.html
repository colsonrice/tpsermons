The Urgent Kingdom

Marriage and Redemption

Reflection sheet

The Urgent Kingdom · Mark 10 · Aaron Brockett, Lead Pastor

The Pharisee Trap

  1. When have I found myself asking questions with an agenda rather than a desire to understand?
  2. Where have I prioritized winning an argument over seeking truth in my relationships?
  3. How do I identify and address heart issues in my own life when faced with conflicts?
  4. Reflect on a time when I chose grace over strict rules in my interactions. What was the outcome?

God's Vision for Marriage

  1. How have my expectations of marriage been influenced by societal norms rather than God's design?
  2. In what ways does my current marriage or significant relationship depict the covenantal love God shows me?
  3. Where am I currently struggling to become 'one' with my spouse or significant other, and what's one step I can take to address it?
  4. Consider a time when I realigned my understanding or practice of marriage with what God intends. What prompted this change?

Hope Beyond Brokenness

  1. Where in my life or family history have I seen redemption transform brokenness into hope?
  2. How have I experienced grace covering my failures, and what does that teach me about God's view on redemption?
  3. When have I witnessed a relationship mend in unexpected ways?
  4. Where do I need to embrace God's forgiveness in the context of broken relationships?
  5. Reflect on a challenging situation that required me to lean into grace. What did I learn from it?

This Week

Identify one area in your marriage or relationships where you'll practice covenantal commitment, and choose a brother here to discuss it with next week.
