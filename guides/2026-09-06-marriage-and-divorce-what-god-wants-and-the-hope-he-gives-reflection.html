Reflection sheet

The Urgent Kingdom · Mark 10 · Aaron Brockett

The Design of Marriage

  1. Where have I treated commitments lightly, and what was the result?
  2. In what ways does my marriage or significant relationship reflect the idea of two becoming one?
  3. How does the notion of marriage as a covenant rather than a contract challenge my current thinking?
  4. What aspects of God's design for marriage have I found most challenging to live out?

Facing the Challenges

  1. Recall a time when societal expectations influenced my relationship choices. What did I learn?
  2. When have I felt tempted to walk away from a commitment, and how did I respond?
  3. How does cultural pressure affect my view or practice of marriage?
  4. What specific cultural pressures have tested the strength of my commitments recently?

Grace and Redemption

  1. Identify a moment when I experienced or witnessed grace in a relationship. What impact did it have?
  2. Where have I needed to forgive or ask for forgiveness, and what was the outcome?
  3. What steps can I take this week to embody grace more fully in my relationships?
  4. Reflect on a time when I saw reconciliation change a situation once thought hopeless. What did it teach me?

This Week

This week, address one area where you have not fully embodied grace in a relationship. Share this with someone who can hold you accountable.

Put it in a note on your phone, then text it to the man who will ask you about it next week.
