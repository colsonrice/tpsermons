Reflection sheet

The Urgent Kingdom · Mark 10 · Aaron Brockett, Lead Pastor

Questioning Marriage

  1. How has my family's story shaped my view of marriage, and how have I seen God's grace in that?
  2. When have I faced questions about my faith with mixed motives, and how did I respond?
  3. Where have I seen hard-heartedness impact my relationships, and what steps am I taking to soften my heart?
  4. Have I felt overwhelmed by relational failure, and how did I hold onto hope?

Marriage's Purpose

  1. Where do I see marriage as a covenant, not a contract, despite societal messages?
  2. How does seeing marriage as a reflection of Christ's commitment challenge how I invest in relationships?
  3. Where have I felt spiritual opposition in my relationships, and how am I fighting it?
  4. Reflect on a time I settled for less in a relationship. What did I learn about God's designs?
  5. How am I nurturing oneness in my relationships, or where might I need to grow?

Hope for the Broken

  1. What past failures haunt me, and where do I need to see God's grace bringing redemption?
  2. Have I experienced God's grace rewriting my story, and what impact has that had on my relationships?
  3. How can I uphold the value of marriage in my current or future relationships despite cultural pressures?
  4. Where am I currently struggling to see hope, and how does Jesus's example challenge that view?

This Week

Identify one step you'll take this week to strengthen your covenantal commitment, and name the man you'll ask to keep you accountable.

Put it in a note on your phone, then text it to the man who will ask you about it next week.
