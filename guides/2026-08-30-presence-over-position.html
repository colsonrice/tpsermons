The Urgent Kingdom

Presence Over Position

August 30, 2026Mark 9:30-50
Message pageWatchListen

Recap

The sermon focused on Mark 9:30-50, where Jesus prepares His disciples for His coming sacrifice and teaches on true greatness. As they argue about who among them is the greatest, Jesus emphasizes that greatness comes from serving others without seeking reward. He warns against becoming territorial about spiritual matters and stresses the seriousness of leading others into sin. Jesus uses metaphors to challenge His followers to cut out sin from their lives. Ultimately, Jesus urges His disciples to value presence with Him over their status or position.

Discuss

True Greatness Defined

  1. How does Jesus' definition of greatness differ from the world's?
  2. What does serving without an agenda look like in everyday life?
  3. When have you seen or practiced greatness as Jesus describes it?

Avoiding Territorialism

  1. Why do you think the disciples were concerned about others using Jesus' name?
  2. How can our focus on group boundaries hinder God's work?
  3. Describe a situation where unity was more important than differences.

Taking Sin Seriously

  1. What might Jesus mean by saying it is better to cut off what causes us to sin?
  2. Why is it important not to tolerate sin, according to Jesus?
  3. Reflect on a 'hidden sin' you need to address. How can you take steps to cut it off?

Take Action

This week, identify one person you can serve anonymously—someone who cannot give anything back to you. This could be paying for someone's coffee, leaving a supportive note, or helping a neighbor with a chore. Focus on the act itself without seeking recognition.

Reflections

  • Think of a time when you prioritized position over presence. What impact did it have?
  • Journal about a fear or insecurity that causes you to cling to position.
  • Reflect on an area where you need to invite God's presence more deeply, rather than seeking recognition.
