# Modern edition: additions

Build everything in the spec above exactly as written, then add these four
things. They sit on top of the classic guide; they do not replace any of it.

**read_refs** — one or two short verse ranges the group actually reads, with
verse numbers: `["Mark 10:13-16"]` or `["Mark 10:13-16", "Mark 10:17-22"]`.
Never a bare chapter. Pick the verses the message turned on, usually four to
ten verses each. A man reading aloud should finish a range in under a minute.

**probes** — for each section, one or two short follow-ups the leader can use
if the room stalls or the answers stay on the surface. They push for the
specific week, the named person, the actual cost. One question mark each.

**obstacle** — a single "Get Honest" question asked near the end of the night:
what will realistically stop *this man* from following through *this week*.
It must contain the word "you". Friction, not theory; never "what makes this
hard in our culture". One question mark.

**carry** — one sentence naming exactly what the group will ask each other
next week, so the commitment actually gets followed up.
